How to use this workbench

All networks here are synthetic benchmark networks. They are teaching cases, not published IEEE test systems or measured grid data.

  1. Pick a preset, then drag the load factor to scale every load bus.
  2. The Newton-Raphson solver recomputes bus voltages, angles, line flows and losses synchronously.
  3. Click a bus or a line to inspect exact computed values. Arrow keys move the selection.
  4. Use the N-1 panel to screen every single-line outage, then export the whole experiment.

The convergence plot shows the real maximum mismatch at each Newton iteration. A red status means the solver honestly reported non-convergence at the voltage-collapse limit.
